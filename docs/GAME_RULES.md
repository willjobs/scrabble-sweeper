# Rules and information

15×15, columns A–O and rows 1–15. Ordinary center H8; opening word of at least two letters must cover it. Later plays connect orthogonally. Place a contiguous horizontal/vertical word; old tiles can bridge new tiles. Every maximal word of length ≥2 must be in the bundled dictionary. Single-tile intersections form and score both words exactly once. Blanks receive permanent A–Z assignments and always have value zero.

100 standard English tiles, unique physical IDs, seven per rack. New DL/TL multiply that letter's value. New DW/TW multiply the whole word multiplicatively, once for each newly formed word containing the square. Occupied premiums never apply again. Seven new tiles add 50 after summing all words. No uncommitted score hints.

Bonuses: DL 24, TL 12, DW 16, TW 8; 165 ordinary. Each cell equals its half-turn counterpart. TW cannot enter the central 5×5 or touch another TW, even diagonally. This does not guarantee a unique deduction from scores.

Score-only exposes turn totals only. Reveal-used additionally shows occupied squares' true types, including ordinary. Empty mirrors remain unrevealed. Notes are private bitmask hypotheses, mirrored automatically, without truth comparison. History reconstructs placed letters and discovery cutoffs; notes/racks are absent.

Pass and exchange score zero. Exchange requires seven tiles available before drawing replacements, then recycles/shuffles selected tiles. Sixth consecutive zero-score action ends the game and deducts each player's remaining rack value. Going out with an empty bag ends immediately: opponent rack sum is deducted from opponent and credited to the player going out. Negative scores and ties are valid. The entire premium board is then public.
